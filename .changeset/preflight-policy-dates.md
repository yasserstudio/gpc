`gpc preflight` policy dates and wording now match Google's October 29, 2026 policy update. Contacts and precise-location rules for apps targeting Android 17 become mandatory on January 27, 2027 (not October 28, 2026), and both findings now say a Play Console declaration is required. The `READ_CALL_LOG` finding no longer says phone-call verification stopped on July 15, 2026; Google's policy makes that change effective January 27, 2027. The geofencing foreground-service finding drops an Android 17 enforcement date that Google never published.
